Series Collectibles


CRYBABY X Carebears Series

The CRYBABY X Carebears Series is their most recent collection. This is a pendant keychain with multiple
colors and different expressions resembling the carebear characters. Along with the keychain, comes a charm attatched to it with a sad face. Some names in the original cartoon are grumpy bear, sunshine bear,and cheer bear.


CRYBABY: Crying Again Series

This is the first series ever to be released in POPMART stores. There is no specific theme to this series but, has empathy and reassurance tied with the titles for each figure.


CRYBABY: Crying For Love

This series could argue it's valentine's or relationship-themed. In this series, each
figure comes with a teeny tiny crying heart that goes with that specific collectible.
