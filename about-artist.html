Crying For A FanPage-About Artist

Molly Yllom

Molly Yllom is a Thai artist and creator of PopMarts blindbox
collectibles named "CryBaby". These cute figures have their signature
large tears and pouty faces. You either love them or find
them ugly but, that's what gives them charm feeling uniqe!

Molly is known to be the first Thai artist to collaborate with POPMART.
POPMART is a Chinese toy company that is known for producing blindbox collectibles.
Blindboxes are a series of collectibles where you don't know what type of item you'll get
until you open the box.

Concept

The origin of these fugly babys starts in 2017 when Mollys dog Somchun passed away. She had a deep connection with her dog makinging it more devistating. When CRYBABY was first made,
she had no intentions of her figures look like babies.
For Molly, it a personification of emotion. She grew up with what sounded like an emotionally unavailable mother.
This caused Molly to suppress her crying growingup upbecause of her mother telling Molly to stop crying.
When Somchun passed, she took inspiration from her dog and made dog-like hair on the CRYBABY
even though it just a bob haircut.
